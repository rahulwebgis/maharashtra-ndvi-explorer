How this portal works

This tool searches the public Sentinel-2 L2A and Landsat 8/9 Collection 2 Level-2 archives through Microsoft's Planetary Computer and computes NDVI live in your browser — no login, no server.

  1. Pick the sensor and Date A / Date B (highlighted calendar days have imagery over your AOI). The closest low-cloud acquisition within ±10 days (widening to ±35) is used; for Sentinel-2, all tiles from that same day covering the AOI are combined.
  2. NDVI = (NIR − Red) / (NIR + Red) is rendered as map tiles (Sentinel-2 B08/B04, Landsat nir08/red). Sentinel-2 scenes from processing baseline 04.00 (2022+) are corrected for the +1000 reflectance offset so years stay comparable.
  3. Choose a District → Taluka → Village (or draw / import a polygon) and click Analyse. Red, NIR and the cloud mask are read pixel-by-pixel from the Cloud-Optimised GeoTIFFs, resampled onto one WGS84 grid, and clouds/shadows are removed (Sentinel-2 SCL classes 3, 8, 9, 10; Landsat QA_PIXEL cloud, dilated cloud and shadow bits).
  4. The dashboard shows mean / median NDVI, vegetation classes, pixel-level change (ΔNDVI = B − A; loss < −0.1, gain > +0.1) and a per-taluka / per-village table. Toggle Δ for the change map and ▦ for the sub-area map.
  5. Export clipped GeoTIFFs (A, B, Δ), a stats CSV, the sub-areas as GeoJSON, the map as PNG, or a print-ready PDF/JPG layout.

Drought reading: NDVI falling between the same season of two years (e.g. October 2023 vs October 2024) signals weaker crop / vegetation cover. Compare the same season, because NDVI follows the crop calendar.

Large AOIs (a whole district) are read at a coarser resolution to stay fast — the dashboard reports the effective pixel size. Built-in boundaries cover all 36 districts, 351 talukas and 45,316 villages (LGD codes); village files load per district on demand. You can replace any layer with your own file.
